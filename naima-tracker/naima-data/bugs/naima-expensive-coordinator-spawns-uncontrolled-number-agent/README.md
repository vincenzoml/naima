# Naima is expensive: the coordinator spawns an uncontrolled number of agents, and a day's work costs too much

Reported by the owner on 2026-10-02. On 2026-10-01 a coordinator ran up to 7 workers at once (one per item or small unit), every one on the strongest model, and hit the spend limit mid-session. What exists: coordinator-and-workers.md says two workers as the standing number, up to four for short cheap work, and a cheaper model for filing, triage, merges and gates, but this is convention only: nothing counts workers, nothing records spend, nothing refuses a fifth. There is no first-class way to group related open items into one unit of work (one worker, one worktree, one branch); naima open can claim several items at once, and the last sweep grouped items into batches by hand. Wanted: a worker cap enforced or at least checked, cost per session recorded as a metric, and items clustered into one unit per worker before staffing.
