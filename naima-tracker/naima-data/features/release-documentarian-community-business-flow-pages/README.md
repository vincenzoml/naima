# Release, documentarian, community and business flow pages

Outward roles have no procedure yet.

Done: one flow page each states the role's queue, what it owns, what it refuses and its hand-off; the release flow proposes a release from the gate and a changelog from items closed since the last one, and asks the owner the `decision`; the community flow routes outside issues and pull requests through the filer and gates; the business flow files licence, funding, sponsorship and citation options as decision items with a recommendation, never acting on them; all are calibrated for open source and say so.
