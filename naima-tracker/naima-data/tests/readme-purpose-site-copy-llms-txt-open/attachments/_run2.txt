3:Naima is a silent software house of AI agents: it turns vibe coding into an exact science. Vibe coding — asking an AI to build software and hoping it worked — leaves work unverified, unrecorded, unrepeatable, uncoordinated and roleless; Naima replaces each with its opposite. You say what you want. Agents build it with industry-grade practice — written [requirements](naima/docs/guide/glossary.md#requirement), tests, [metrics](naima/docs/guide/glossary.md#metric), reviews, [gates](naima/docs/guide/glossary.md#gate) before anything ships — and with [formal methods](naima/docs/guide/glossary.md#formal-methods), properties of the design proven by tools, all applied for you without your needing to know them. Every [claim](naima/docs/guide/glossary.md#claim) comes with its [evidence](naima/docs/guide/glossary.md#evidence): nothing counts as done until it is shown to be. You only decide.
